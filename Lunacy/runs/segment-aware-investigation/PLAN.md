Three fresh Luna xhigh scouts independently inspect repository reality and the latest real MSFT artifacts. Read-only except unique proposal reports. Gate: all proposals identify the smallest sound extension, exact maintained surfaces, invariants, risks, proof, and size.

## Phase 2 — synthesis, implementation, live proof

One fresh Luna max worker judges proposals, selects the simplest existing-pipeline extension, implements it, adds proportional tests, runs all user-required verification, and runs two actual saved MSFT segment findings end to end. Writes one immutable implementation report plus compact evidence/log artifacts. No commit/merge or financial-state writes.

## Phase 3 — simplicity and integrity adversary

One fresh Luna xhigh adversary attacks unnecessary complexity, financial/evidence integrity, leakage, allocations/plugs, persistence drift, and consolidated regression. It may make only clear behavior-preserving simplifications; correctness defects require a fresh repair step. Terminal report required.

## Phase 4 — fresh read-only product reviewer and parent gate

Because this task explicitly requires a fresh reviewer and is cross-cutting/high-risk, launch one fresh Luna xhigh read-only reviewer after the write barrier closes. It inspects the exact saved scan/query/evidence/result artifacts and consolidated boundary; no broad-suite replay. Parent then inspects targeted final diff/code and bounded acceptance proof and returns `PASS`, `PASS WITH SMALL FIXES`, or `DO NOT MERGE`; user-facing verdict is `PASS` or `DO NOT MERGE`.

## Ownership and safety

All source/test/live-artifact writes are serialized through Phase 2, any explicitly authorized repair, or bounded Phase 3 simplification. Preserve prior dirty/untracked Hive runs and unrelated data. No external writes, commit, merge, adjustment history, approved-state mutation, or new pipeline.
