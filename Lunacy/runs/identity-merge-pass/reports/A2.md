## Findings

### A2-1 [P1] duplicate-concept selector drift still permits a competing key to auto-approve

`_history_identity_lookup()` blocks a competing key only when the complete
`target_row_key` matches, and detects selector drift only when `item_key` also
matches (`src/smrik_fund/main.py:358-389`). For a duplicated standard concept,
the selector is normally label-qualified. A fresh `.venv` probe used an
approved `standard_concept:SellingGeneralAndAdminExpenses|label:Sales and
marketing` row, then renamed that P&L label to `Marketing` and supplied a
different item key. The new key became
`standard_concept:SellingGeneralAndAdminExpenses|label:Marketing` and lookup
returned `new`.

The same probe produced `possible_duplicate=False` and
`aggregate_over_adjustment=False`; with a strong accepting review,
`evaluate_risk_gate()` returned `auto_approve`. Thus a duplicate source row
whose presentation metadata drifts can false-split and stack a competing
economic event, contrary to the occupied-row contract in
`docs/ai_fund_v1_section_2_implementation_spec.md:934-941` and the aggregate
guard. Require a stable duplicate-row selector or fail closed on any occupied
concept/period whose qualified selector changes, regardless of item key.

### A2-2 [P2] malformed v2 history can omit the persisted row-selector snapshot

`validated_history_identity_rows()` treats an absent history column as an
implicit match (`src/smrik_fund/ingestion/adjustments.py:123-141`). A fresh
probe removed `target_row_key` from an otherwise valid v2 approved row:
validation returned non-`None`, and `resolve_current_adjustments()` accepted
the row by reconstructing `target_row_key` from `candidate_identity`. This
conflicts with the explicit persisted row-selector requirement and “malformed
v2 ... fail closed” contract
(`docs/ai_fund_v1_section_2_implementation_spec.md:939-948`).
