## Design and Section 2 mapping

`evaluate_risk_gate(candidate, review, conditions)` is pure and makes no model
call or financial judgment. `RiskGateConditions` carries only precomputed
mechanical facts: materiality eligibility, reconciliation, duplicate/group/
aggregate checks, source-target availability/sign, individual/zero-target
over-adjustment, and the all-mechanics result. Missing (`None`) facts fail
closed. No materiality threshold is invented. Reviewer verdict, evidence,
judgment, target/period validity, amount presence/basis, and calculation-valid
when calculated are enforced directly from the established Task 7/8 models.
Disclosed basis does not require a calculation result.

The ordered reasons cover Reviewer revise/reject, weak evidence, null/nonfinite/
negative amount, estimated/unknown or disagreeing basis, judgment/target/period/
calculation failures, and every mechanical condition above. Result properties
make both approval eligibility and human-review requirement explicit.
