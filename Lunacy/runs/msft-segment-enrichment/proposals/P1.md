## Approach

1. Add one focused `src/smrik_fund/ingestion/segments.py`. Query the existing filing’s XBRL facts by the generic `StatementBusinessSegmentsAxis`; retain rows with exactly that one axis (exclude additional `dim_*` axes), duration periods matching the selected analytical FY end dates, and only the generic US-GAAP Revenue and OperatingIncomeLoss concepts. Keep raw `concept`, `label`, axis/member QNames, `dimension_member_label`, `value`, unit/currency, `fact_id`, `context_ref`, period start/end, accession and source URL. Never synthesize a Microsoft segment name; missing member labels, duplicate `(axis, member, concept, period)` candidates, or unsafe period matches remain unresolved.
2. Produce a long `segment_analytics` frame at `(segment member, metric, period)` grain. Raw values remain untouched. Add only deterministic columns: absolute YoY, guarded growth (same strictly-positive endpoint rule as `prepare_pnl`), revenue share and share change (bps), contribution to consolidated revenue growth, operating margin and margin change (bps), and comparable operating-income-growth contribution. Zero/missing/sign-changing endpoints yield `N/A`/null, never zero or a guessed rate.
3. Persist two small, separate outputs under `data/<TICKER>/03_output/`: `segment_analytics.csv` for source rows plus derived metrics and `segment_reconciliation_checks.csv` for `(metric, period)` aggregate checks. Reconciliation records reported segment total, reported consolidated total from canonical P&L, residual (`consolidated - segment total`), member coverage and status `PASS`, `NOT_DIRECTLY_COMPARABLE`, or `UNRESOLVED`. Sum only complete, unique comparable members; preserve any residual and never plug it.
4. Extend scan formatting with an optional segment frame. Give each `(segment member, metric)` a stable `line_ref=S##` (sorted by source axis/member/metric), leaving all existing `L##` assignment/meaning compatible. Add a compact segment section: revenue values/growth/share/share-change/revenue-growth contribution; operating-income values/growth/margin/margin bps/contribution; one short reconciliation summary. Validate the union of L/S refs; unknown or invented refs fail closed. Change only the prompt wording needed to say supplied reportable segments may be compared with consolidated performance; no names or conclusions are seeded.
5. In `analyze --scan`, extract and save segments from the same filing already attached to the freshly built P&L before calling the existing scan. If no filing/segment facts are available in a synthetic or local call, retain the consolidated-only path; a real run must not silently claim segment enrichment. Do not invoke this path for adjustments/review.

## Finance invariants

Preserve source labels, signs, missingness, periods, units, accession and fact/context provenance. No undisclosed EBITDA/profit metric, allocation, forecasting, adjustment, history restatement, geography, 10-Q support, taxonomy ontology, dimensional model, retrieval or new LLM role. Ambiguous comparability is visible and blocks derived metrics.

## Risks, complexity, and recommendation

Main risks are multi-axis facts being double-counted, member labels/QNames not aligning across periods, and duplicate contexts that look numerically identical. Fail closed and retain candidates/metadata. Estimated change: one focused module (~150–190 production lines), small CLI/formatter/prompt edits, and one focused test module plus scan assertions; moderate-low complexity. Recommend this design: separate grains keep canonical P&L/adjustment identity untouched while the scan receives only compact, source-backed context.
