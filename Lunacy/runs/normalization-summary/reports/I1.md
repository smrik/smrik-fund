## Decision

- Chosen C: deterministic exact-key grouping plus compact presentation.
- A rejected: the one-candidate Reviewer already records evidence concerns; a
  prompt/schema change would not safely synthesize sibling-period context.
- B rejected: extra model authority, cost, latency, failure/cache surface, and
  risk of conflating assessment with verdict/gate/application state.
- D rejected: no measured need for a Reviewer change after inspecting the live
  run; keep the Reviewer contract frozen.

## Implemented

- `build_normalization_summary()` groups by explicit `group_id`, else exact
  normalized `(topic, target_line, sub_item)` key, preserving candidate rows,
  periods, amounts/nulls, reasons, uncertainty, concerns, gate reasons, and
  separate workflow status lists.
- Existing manifest now stores `normalization_summary` with schema version
  `normalization-summary-v1`; no new artifact/history schema or model call.
- Integrated CLI prints item/line, per-period supported amounts, financial
  assessment, reviewer/evidence profile, unresolved issues, why-not-automatic,
  and distinct Reviewer/gate/final/application statuses. Full evidence remains
  in existing artifacts; Analyst/Reviewer artifact paths remain visible.
- Display flags make `plausible_candidate`, `amount_unresolved`, weak/strong
  Reviewer evidence, and `repeated_period_evidence` distinct; recurrence is not
  asserted and no amount is summed or inferred.
